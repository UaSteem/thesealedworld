# Bandit family visual contract

Approved direction: raster PNG with real transparency. Arrow SVG trials are separate experiments, not the current asset workflow.

Canonical references:
- png/bandit_thug-v1.png: ordinary minion, baseline illustrated style.
- png/bandit_raider-v1.png: veteran equipment quality.
- png/bandit_captain-v1.png: elite equipment quality.

Preserve the same clean dark outlines, restrained angular cel shading, broad readable shapes,
muted brown leather, ochre/olive-grey cloth and charcoal steel. Natural human skin with clear
coherent facial shading. Adult human proportions and expressive uncovered faces.
Do not introduce painterly realism, photorealism, pixel art, chibi proportions, gloss or busy textures.
Individual faces, builds and poses must differ while the rendering style stays consistent.

One full-body character per transparent square image; complete weapon and feet with margins.
Opponent should face left for the right side of combat; display mirroring is allowed.
Face is the first detail priority, then silhouette/hands/equipment, minor wear last.

Rank progression:
- Minion: cheap patched leather and plain weapons.
- Veteran: serviceable reinforced leather, modest metal protection, durable equipment.
- Elite: coordinated good stolen equipment, mail/brigandine or role-appropriate protection.
- Boss: strongest distinct silhouette and best practical equipment, not a royal costume.
Preserve role-specific loadout and check the current catalog before drawing.
Human outlaws, without the retired settler-origin framing. Magic is planned; the accepted hedge-wizard artwork remains.

Workflow: one candidate at a time, show user, apply corrections if requested, mark accepted
only after approval. Keep originals and alternatives. Use accepted PNGs as style references;
never copy the same face onto every role. No Quiver calls without explicit authorization.
