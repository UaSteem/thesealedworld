# Goblin tribe — individual descriptions

Use master_prompt.txt first. Attach the accepted goblin_sneak.svg as a style reference. Send one description per generation.

## 01. goblin_sneak

```text
A small wiry goblin sneak wearing a worn hooded cloak and light leather armor. Two sharp daggers, one in each hand. Low cautious stalking posture, watchful eyes and a sly threatening grin. Face and both daggers clearly visible.
```

## 02. goblin_scavenger

```text
A goblin scavenger carrying a huge overflowing backpack of salvaged tools, torn cloth and scrap metal. A simple club in one hand and a small battered buckler in the other. Bent under the load, possessive grin, patched leather clothing. The backpack is the defining silhouette; avoid tiny clutter.
```

## 03. goblin_cutter

```text

Exposed face, tense long fingers, blades held low. Distinguish him from the hooded sneak with a shorter ragged outfit and a sharper attacking silhouette.
```

