Build the illustration in this priority order:

1. Establish the complete character silhouette, body proportions and pose.
2. Fully resolve the uncovered face and pointed ears. The face is the highest
   detail priority: clear eyes, nose, mouth, expression and distinct goblin features.
3. Add the main armor shapes, weapon and throne.
4. Add secondary shading, wear, rivets, scratches and small decorations last.

If the complexity budget is limited, simplify armor decoration and the throne.
Always preserve a complete, expressive, readable face.
Never hide the face behind a helmet or replace facial features with vague shapes.
