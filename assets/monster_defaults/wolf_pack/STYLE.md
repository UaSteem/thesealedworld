# Wolf family style
Transparent PNGs, clean dark outlines, restrained angular cel shading and broad fur shapes.
Ash-grey, charcoal and warm bone-grey base palette; amber eyes. Natural quadruped wolf anatomy.
Muzzle and eyes first, coherent silhouette and limbs next, sparse fur details last.
Opponents face left. One full-body wolf, complete paws, ears and tail, no platform or background.
No fine hair texture, photorealism, painterly rendering, clothes or humanoid anatomy.
First candidate cub-v1.png pending approval; later variants use the accepted wolf as the style reference.
Rank differences use build, posture, age and scars; special themes only when specified by catalog.
